VICE CITY HOOD NEWS

GTA 6 news and gameplay shorts.

VICE CITY HOOD NEWS is a fan channel that makes short videos about Grand Theft Auto VI: news from official sources, clearly labelled rumors, and clips from our own gameplay.

The VCHN app

We run a small private tool (“VCHN”) on our own computer to produce the channel. With our permission it connects to our own YouTube channel to:

  • see the gameplay videos we uploaded privately to our channel,
  • download those videos of ours to cut short clips from them,
  • upload finished shorts to the same channel.

It only ever works with our own channel. It is not offered to other users. Details are in the privacy policy.
